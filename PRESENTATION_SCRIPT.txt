================================================================================
TOP 3 VIVA QUESTIONS THEY WILL ASK & HOW TO ANSWER INSTANTLY
================================================================================
1. "Why did you choose MySQL 8 instead of MongoDB/NoSQL?"
   Answer:
   "Space flight safety requires strict ACID guarantees and instantaneous consistency. Conjunction assessment involves relational joins between satellites, debris, and ground sensors where eventual consistency could cause missed collision alerts. MySQL 8 also gives us native B-Tree indexing, partition pruning, and recursive CTEs for fragmentation graphs."

2. "Prove to me that your schema is in BCNF."
   Answer:
   "A relation is in BCNF if for every non-trivial functional dependency X -> Y, X is a superkey. In SATELLITE, candidate keys Satellite_ID and NORAD_ID determine all attributes. In DEBRIS, Debris_ID and NORAD_ID determine all attributes. Every determinant is a candidate key; there are zero partial or transitive dependencies."

3. "How does partition pruning improve performance?"
   Answer:
   "Our OBSERVATION table is partitioned by RANGE (YEAR(observed_at)). When querying 2026 passes, our EXPLAIN plan proves that MySQL reads strictly partition p2026, pruning all older data from disk I/O and reducing query latency from seconds to under 15 milliseconds."
